About me

Learning, building, shipping

I am an Information Technology student at Limkokwing University, currently in semester 3. I love building web apps that work well on phones, because that is how most people in Sierra Leone get online. My biggest project so far is SL Connect, a platform that helps people find jobs, workers, houses to rent and land for sale. I also built Game Boi, a gaming platform made for players who want to play, compete and win.

SL Connect

An installable web app (PWA) for finding work, workers, houses for rent and land for sale in Sierra Leone. Built to be fast, simple and perfect on a phone screen.
